Robin Cerutti is a Canadian photographer and visual artist whose captivating work explores the boundaries between reality and imaginary worlds. His poetic and enigmatic photographs — series Mirror, Parallel, and Parts of Life — transport us into dreamlike universes that feel remarkably real. Without any digital alteration, he uses real elements — light, water, materials, and the human body — to create images that may appear digital at first glance, yet are entirely photographic.

His work has been exhibited in Canada and internationally, including the United States, France, Italy, Belgium, Mexico, and China.
