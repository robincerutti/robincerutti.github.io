Robin Cerutti is a Franco-Canadian photographer and visual artist. His work commences in reality and fades into a dialogue between evidence and wonder — images that appear impossible yet are entirely photographic, captured through an in-camera vision where what exists is what you see. His positioning: <em>totally real surrealism</em>.
 
At the heart of his practice is the human — the encounter, the portrait, the raw truth of a person. Whether through conceptual series that deconstruct perception, documentary work rooted in real communities, or commercial and editorial photography, Robin seeks that rare moment where inner life breaks through the surface. A background in science and engineering sharpens his understanding of light and physical phenomena, without defining his art.
 
His work has been exhibited internationally in Canada, the United States, France, Italy, Belgium, Switzerland, China, and Mexico.
