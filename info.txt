Robin Cerutti is a Franco-Canadian photographer and visual artist. His work explores the tension between reality and surrealism — through entirely in-camera techniques, no digital manipulation, no post-production alteration. Using water as his primary visual medium, he captures optical phenomena — reflection, diffraction, surface distortion — to create images that appear impossible yet are entirely photographic. His positioning: <em>totally real surrealism</em>.

His practice spans fine art and commercial photography, artistic direction, and set design. A background in science and corporate engineering informs his precise understanding of light and physical phenomena, without defining his art.

His work has been exhibited internationally in Canada, the United States, France, Italy, Belgium, Switzerland, China, and Mexico.
