<p><strong>Enk Studio — Stereo Portraits</strong></p>

<p>
Portrait series shot on location in Enk Studio’s office using a 4-lens stereoscopic analogue camera system (Nishika-type).
</p>

<p>
Studio lighting was used in a controlled setup. The resulting multi-view captures were edited into looping GIFs that cycle through the full parallax range and back, creating subtle spatial motion through shifting perspectives.
</p>
