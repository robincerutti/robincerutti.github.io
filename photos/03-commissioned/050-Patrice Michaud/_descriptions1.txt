Grand voyage désorganisé — Patrice Michaud (2021)
Art direction and graphic design: Éloïse Deschamps
Photography: Robin Cerutti
Styling: Mélanie Brisson
Makeup and hair: Arianne Tremblay

The visual universe of Grand voyage désorganisé was developed as a collaborative project built around themes of space exploration and nostalgia.

Inspired by the 1977 launch of the Voyager I and II probes — often described as humanity’s “message in a bottle” sent into the universe — the imagery explores a dialogue between distance, time, and intimacy.

The project reflects a tension between nostalgic memory and the desire for discovery, understood as an inner journey rather than a purely external one.

For the televised special Dernière escale d’un grand voyage désorganisé (2025), the visual language was extended into a more saturated aesthetic, drawing from 1970s to 1990s pop culture, with influences from funk and rock imagery.
