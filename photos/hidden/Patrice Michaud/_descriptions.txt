Patrice Michaud 

he artistic direction and graphic design for Patrice Michaud's album Grand voyage désorganisé (2021) were created by Éloïse Deschamps.
Primary Creative Team
The visual universe of this project is the result of a collaboration between several creators:
Art Direction and Graphic Design: Éloïse Deschamps
Photography: Robin Cerutti
Styling: Mélanie Brisson
Makeup and Hair: Arianne Tremblay
Visual Concepts and Influences
The aesthetics of the album and subsequent tour revolve around spatial and nostalgic themes:
Space Inspiration: The concept is deeply tied to the 1977 launch of the Voyager I and II probes, viewed as humanity's "message in a bottle" to the universe.
Relationship with Time: The visuals evoke a quest for openness toward others and an exploration of one's "inner country," blending happy nostalgia with a journey into the unknown.
Scenic Evolution: For the televised special Dernière escale d’un grand voyage désorganisé (2025), art director Marcella Grimaux pushed the aesthetic toward a colorful universe influenced by the 1970s through the 1990s, incorporating funk and rock vibes.
