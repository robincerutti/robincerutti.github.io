Parts of Life is a photographic series that captures candid and fleeting moments from around the world, begun in 2006. The focus of the series is on the emotional energy and narratives conveyed by the subjects, rather than the physical setting. The goal is to convey a sense of imbalance and spontaneity, capturing the moments that could never be posed or staged. Through the lens, I aim to give viewers an intimate and personal perspective on the human experience and to encourage introspection and self-reflection. The series is a visual diary of the different stories and emotions that I have encountered during my travels and it invites the viewer to question themselves and their own life experiences.
