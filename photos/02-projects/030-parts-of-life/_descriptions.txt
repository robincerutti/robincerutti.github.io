“Parts of Life” is a photographic series I began in 2006, built from candid moments encountered in different places around the world. Rather than focusing on location alone, the work is driven by the emotional energy, tension, and quiet narratives carried by the people and situations within each frame. The series embraces imbalance, spontaneity, and the fragile intensity of moments that could never be staged, offering an intimate perspective on the human experience. Over time, it has become a visual diary of the stories and emotions I have crossed paths with, inviting viewers to find echoes of their own lives in these fleeting fragments.
